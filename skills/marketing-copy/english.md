# English

Rules drawn from the persuasion and credibility research, Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing"
catalogue, and pages of engineering companies whose copy works. Sources in
`research/english_sources.md`.

## Clichés

Delete on sight, or replace with the fact the word was hiding.

| Kind | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Empty novelty | next-generation, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, revolutionary, game-changing |
| Empty ease | seamless, effortless, frictionless, out of the box |
| Empty scale | limitless, infinite possibilities, at scale, world-class, unparalleled |
| Model vocabulary | unlock, empower, elevate, harness, delve, showcase, tapestry, testament, pivotal |
| Borrowed intimacy | your journey, we've got you covered, built for you |
| Mission filler | shaping the future, redefining what's possible, pushing the boundaries |
| Fake openness | anyone can, for everyone, open to all |

## Structures

- **Stacked declaratives.** "We simulate the world at real scale. Our engine draws on the WGS84
  ellipsoid at true scale. The four libraries beneath it are open source." Three sentences, one
  shape, one piece of jargon, nothing a reader can picture. Merge into one sentence that carries
  a fact.
- **"Not just X, but Y".** Say Y.
- **"From X to Y".** "From games to digital twins" names two categories and commits to neither.
- **Question hero.** "What if you could simulate anything?" The reader did not ask.
- **Colon reveal.** "One engine. One goal: reality." Drop the drum roll and state the fact.
- **Em dash.** At most one per page of copy, never in a hero.

## Voice

First person plural for the company, names for the founders. Present tense for what exists,
future tense only with a date or the word "planned". Contractions are fine; readers take them
as a human writing.

## Before and after

> Before: We simulate the world at real scale. Our engine draws on the WGS84 ellipsoid at true
> scale. The four libraries beneath it are open source and anyone can read the code.
> After: In SushiEngine you can travel from Pluto to Mercury in milliseconds. [video]

> Before: A next-generation simulation platform empowering industries and gamers to unlock
> limitless possibilities.
> After: SushiEngine models the solar system. [Game or customer] runs on it today.

> Before: Our world-class team is passionate about pushing the boundaries of what's possible.
> After: Mustafa and Mahmut Yavuz Garip started writing the engine in [year]. [One line each on
> what they built.]
