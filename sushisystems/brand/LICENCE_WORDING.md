# Licence wording in Sushi Systems copy

The `marketing-copy` skill says copy names a licence exactly. This page says what the Sushi Systems
licences are.

Sushi Systems libraries are source-available: free for non-commercial use, with a paid licence for
commercial use. Copy says exactly that. It never says "open source" or "açık kaynak" about a Sushi
product, because those words promise commercial use. SushiEngine and the website are closed, and
copy that mentions their source says so.

The licence text and the holder are in `../licence/LICENCE.md`.
